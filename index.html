01 • EST. 1939 • WORLD'S #1 SELLING BISCUIT

Parle-G Gold

First baked in Vile Parle in 1939 as Parle Gluco to bring wholesome nourishment to every Indian home. Crafted from sun-ripened Indian wheat, pure skimmed milk solids, and golden sugar syrup, stamped with 16 steam-release pinholes and a 26-point scalloped crown.

ORIGIN Vile Parle, 1939
PALATE Roasted Wheat & Malt
ARCHITECTURE 26 Scallops • 16 Vents
RITUAL 3.2s Hot Chai Dip
02 • EST. 1996 • PLATINA RESERVE

Hide & Seek Milano

Debuted in 1996 as India's very first moulded chocolate chip cookie. Its signature diagonal fluted ridges cradle over 28% rich dark Belgian-style chocolate chips, allowing you to nibble crisp cocoa shortbread row by row along the diagonal grooves.

ORIGIN Platina Line, 1996
PALATE 28% Dark Choco Chips
CRAFT Diagonal Moulded Flutes
RITUAL Trace the Ridges
03 • EST. 1983 • CONFECTIONERY ICON

Melody Chocolaty

The twin-layered confection that sparked India's most famous question: "Melody itni chocolaty kyun hai?" Slow-cooked golden butterscotch caramel forms a chewy outer shell around a dense, velvety dark chocolate truffle ganache core.

ORIGIN Vile Parle, 1983
EXTERIOR Butterscotch Caramel
INNER CORE Dark Cocoa Truffle
SIGNATURE Dual-Core Confection
04 • EST. 1942 & 1972 • SAVORY & SWEET CRACKERS

Monaco & Krackjack

Pioneers of Indian high-tea crackers: Monaco (1942) is laminated eighteen times for a feather-light, sea-salted golden puff, while Krackjack (1972) balances crystalline sugar glaze with brisk savory salt in one unmistakable snap.

ORIGIN 1942 & 1972 Classics
PALATE Sweet & Sea-Salted
CRAFT 18-Fold Lamination
RITUAL High-Tea Canapés
05 • EST. 1929 & 1963 • BOTANICAL SWEETHOUSE

Kaccha Mango & Kismi

Direct descendants of Mohanlal Dayal Chauhan's founding 1929 Swadeshi candy workshop. Translucent emerald-green spun sugar captures the sharp tang of raw Indian Kairi mangoes, alongside Kismi's slow-simmered Malabar cardamom milk toffee.

ORIGIN 1929 Founding Atelier
PALATE Raw Kairi & Elaichi
CRAFT Spun Sugar & Caramel
HERITAGE Swadeshi Sweethouse
03 / HERITAGE

Born in Vile Parle, 1929.

Founded by Mohanlal Dayal Chauhan with twelve artisans and a Swadeshi vision to craft world-class confectionery in India.

1929

The First Confectionery Atelier

Mohanlal Dayal Chauhan establishes the House of Parle in Vile Parle, Mumbai.

"Crafted by Indians, for every Indian home."
"Wholesome nourishment for all."
1939

Parle Gluco Is Baked

The first golden glucose biscuits emerge from Parle ovens, democratizing a former colonial luxury.

1982

Reborn as Parle-G

An icon in red and gold becomes the heartbeat of Indian mornings and tea-time rituals.

"G Mane Genius."
"Over one billion packs crafted every month."

World's #1 Biscuit Legacy
